Consider a scenario where you are working for a retail company, and you have been given the task to analyze the sales performance of different products based on their sales quantities and 
total revenue generated. Write a SQL query that utilizes window functions, specifically `RANK()` and `SUM() OVER()`, to rank products based on their sales quantities and 
calculate the cumulative revenue for each product. The resulting output should display the product name, sales quantity rank, total sales quantity, and cumulative revenue.
**Data Schema:**
Assume you have two tables:
1. `products`: Contains product information.
   - Columns: `product_id`, `product_name`
2. `sales`: Contains sales data for each product.
   - Columns: `product_id`, `sales_quantity`, `sales_date`, `unit_price`


-- inputs

product_id,product_name
101,Smart Phone
102,Laptop
103,Computer


product_id,sales_quantity,unit_price,sales_date
101,10,25,2024-01-01
102,5,100,2024-01-02
101,20,25,2024-01-03
103,15,50,2024-01-04


 -- output
product_name,sales_quantity_rank,total_quantity,cumulative_revenue
Smart Phone,1,30,750
Computer,2,15,1500
Laptop,3,5,2000

with sales_stg as (
   select *, (sales_quantity * unit_price) as total_sale_value
   from sales
),
with sales_agg as (
   select product_id, sum(sales_quantity) as total_quantity, sum(total_sale_value) as cumulative_revenue
   from sales_stg
   group by product_id
),
with sales_report as (
   select *, RANK(total_quantity) OVER (ORDER BY total_quantity DESC) as sales_quantity_rank
   from sales_agg s_agg
   join products prdt on s_agg.product_id = prdt.product_id
)
select product_name,sales_quantity_rank,total_quantity,cumulative_revenue FROM sales_report order by sales_quantity_rank
